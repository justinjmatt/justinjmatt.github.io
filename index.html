Justin James Matt

Research Scientist · NASA Langley Research Center

Justin J. Matt is a researcher at NASA Langley Research Center in the Vehicle Dynamics and Control Branch. He received his B.S. and M.S. in aerospace engineering from the University of Kansas in 2020 and 2022. His research interests include system identification, flight testing and wind tunnel testing, flight dynamics modeling, flight controls and handling qualities, and estimation applied to flight dynamics problems.
